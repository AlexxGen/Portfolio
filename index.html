Games

Karkinos Ascension

- Unreal -

Karkinos' Ascension takes place in the lost city of Atlantis, where you play as a crab trying to return to your home in the constellation Cancer. This game was developed in eight weeks as a part of RPI's Game Development 1 class by a team of eight people. We presented this game at GDC 2025!

I made the water and sand shaders, level designed the world including the imported models and terrain using Unreal's terrain tool, and put together the sand and bubble particle systems.

March 2025

Happy Medium

- Unity -

Happy Medium is a gold-rush themed detective thriller. You play as a forgetful medium trying to solve the mystery of what happened to in the town Whiskey Ridge. This game was created in six weeks as a part of RPI's Game Development 1 class by a team of six people.

I made the in hand inventory system, level designed the world including the imported models and terrain using Unity's terrain tool, puzzle designed, and made the opening cut scene using Unity's timeline.

November 2024

Unzipped

- Unity, NavMeshPlus -

Unzipped follows a teddy bear that helps a child navigate through their fears. This was a semester-long project made by Shower Effect Entertainment, a team of 8, for the Game Development II class.

I designed and laid out the levels, lit the scenes using Unity's lighting tools, and programmed the enemies with the help of Unity's behavior trees and NavMeshPlus.
