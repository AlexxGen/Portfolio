Unzipped

- Unity, NavMeshPlus -

Unzipped follows a teddy bear that helps a child navigate through their fears. This was a semester-long project made by Shower Effect Entertainment, a team of 8, for the Game Development II class.

I designed and laid out the levels, lit the scenes using Unity's lighting tools, and programmed the enemies with the help of Unity's behavior trees and NavMeshPlus.
