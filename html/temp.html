Art

Exterior Shot

- Houdini, Nuke, Maya, Adobe Substance Painter -

An exterior nature scene may seem easier to light than an interior because there is only one light source, the sun. However, to effectively paint a 3D world with lights to portray the exact exterior as intended takes so many more lights than just a dome light to represent the sun. I would not have learned that if not for this project.
