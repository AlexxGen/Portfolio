Space with Everything in It

- Houdini, Maya, Adobe Substance Painter -

For my Lighting, Rendering, and Cinema class, I had to compose a scene using all of the models that I have ever made before and light it in a way that tells a story. This is the first project where I had used the Houdini software, which proved to be a steep learning curve. However, within the span of two weeks I was able to extrapolate what I needed from the software to achieve my desired scenes.
